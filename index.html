How to Play Carrom

  • Drag the striker to aim your shot
  • Adjust power using the slider
  • Pocket your color pieces (white or black)
  • Pocket the red queen and cover it with your piece
  • First to pocket all their pieces wins!
  • Foul: Pocketing opponent's piece or striker
