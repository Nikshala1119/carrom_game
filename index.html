How to Play Carrom Pool

  • Step 1: Drag striker left/right to position it on baseline
  • Step 2: Tap anywhere to start aiming - cue stick appears
  • Step 3: Drag in any direction to aim
  • Step 4: Drag back further for more power - power bar shows strength
  • Step 5: Release to shoot!
  • Goal: Pocket all your color pieces (white)
  • Bonus: Pocket red queen and cover it with your piece for +50 points
  • Foul: Pocketing opponent's piece or striker
  • You get another turn when you pocket your piece
